what it does

01 — presets

One command to punish, every time.

Define offences once — name, type, duration. Staff run /offend <player> <offence> and it's done. No typing durations on the fly, no inconsistent lengths, no arguments between mods about what the ban should be.

02 — audit trail

Every punishment tracked forever.

Every ban and mute gets a unique ID, storing the player, moderator, offence, duration, issue time, expiry, and status. Nothing gets lost. Nothing gets disputed. Look any punishment up with /offend lookup <id>.

03 — voice mutes

Mutes that hold in voice chat too.

Deep integration with Simple Voice Chat. Muted players are silenced in proximity and group voice — not just text. A mute warning fires on their screen when they try to speak, with a 5-second cooldown so it isn't spammy.

04 — mod call system

Players reach staff in seconds.

Players run /modcall [reason] and every online mod gets a clickable alert. The first to click claims it — race-condition safe using atomic compare-and-set so two mods can never both accept the same call.

05 — modcheck room

Bring suspects in for questioning.

Set a private room with /modcheck setup. Pull any player into it with /modcheck <player> — their return location is saved and restored the moment you free them. Sessions survive server restarts.

06 — discord

Rich logs, Discord-native timestamps.

Every ban, mute, unban, and unmute sends a colour-coded embed with Discord-native <t:…> timestamps, full punishment details, and moderator info. Each log type can be toggled independently in config.

all commands

/offend <player> <offence>Apply a saved offence preset. Add broadcast to announce the punishment server-wide.
/unoffend <id>Remove a specific punishment by its unique ID — bans or mutes. Logs the removal to Discord and reverts DiscordSRV role sync.
/offend lookup <id>Pull the full record for any punishment — player, moderator, offence, duration, status, timestamps, and UUIDs.
/offend add <name> <ban|mute> <duration>Create or update an offence preset. Duration supports s / m / h / d / w combinations or perm.
/offend remove <offence>Delete a saved offence preset. Punishments already issued with it are untouched.
/offend listList every saved offence preset with its punishment type and duration.
/offend <player> unban / unmuteRemove a ban or mute by player name. Marks the history record revoked and fires a Discord log.
/offend activate <code>Activate your licence key on this server. Check status anytime with /offend license.
/offend reloadHot-reload messages.yml to update ban screens and mute warnings without restarting. Invalid YAML is rejected safely.
/modcall [reason]Send a help request to all online moderators with a 30-second cooldown. Mods receive a clickable, claimable alert.
/modclaim <id>Accept a mod call. Atomic — only one mod can claim each call. All other mods are notified who picked it up.
/modcheck setup / <player> / free <player>Set the private room in one command, then teleport any player into it. Their return location is saved and restored the moment you free them.
/mod add / remove / listManage the moderator list — controls who receives mod call pings and has access to staff commands.
