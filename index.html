ModCall

staff utility

Players reach staff in one command.

When a player needs help — griefers, bugs, being stuck — they run /modcall and every online moderator is instantly alerted. A staff member accepts and teleports directly to them. No hunting, no DMs, no chaos in chat.

  • /modcall
  • instant staff alerts
  • accept & teleport
  • organised queue

Sussy

A silent queue for suspected cheaters.

Tracks GrimAC violations and unusual straight-down mining patterns, then surfaces flagged players in a private staff-only menu. Inspect, spectate, and review without the player ever knowing. Helps staff gather real evidence before acting — no false bans.

  • /sussy
  • GrimAC integration
  • mining analysis
  • spectate mode
  • silent — no alerts
  • evidence first
